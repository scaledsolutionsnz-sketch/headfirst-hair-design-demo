About the salon

A salon that feels like catching up with a friend.

Headfirst Hair Design is a friendly Christchurch salon built around one idea: you should leave loving your hair and the time you spent in the chair. We keep things calm and unhurried, so there's always room for a proper chat about what you're after.

Whether it's a fresh cut, a colour refresh or a full balayage transformation, we tailor every appointment to your hair and your lifestyle — and we'll always be honest about what will work for you.

  • Locally owned and run, right here in Christchurch
  • A consult before every appointment — no guesswork
  • Quality products that keep your hair healthy between visits
