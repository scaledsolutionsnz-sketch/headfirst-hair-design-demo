Privacy Policy

How we collect and look after your information. This is placeholder content for demonstration.

Headfirst Hair Design respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or book an appointment, and how we use it. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 (NZ).

1. What we collect

  • Your name and contact details when you make an enquiry or booking.
  • The service you're interested in and any notes you share with us.
  • Feedback you choose to send us through the website.

2. How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, manage your bookings, and provide our services. We may contact you about your appointment or to follow up on feedback you've sent.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Enquiries submitted through our forms are delivered to us by email via a third-party form service purely so we can read and respond to them.

4. Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and retain it only as long as needed to provide our services or as required by law.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request that we correct it. To do so, get in touch using the details below.
